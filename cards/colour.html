Left alone on purpose

Recordbench's fixed tone, kind and series colours (alert #a3231f, open #2b5c8a, settled #1f6b45 …) are data encodings, not brand. They stay product-local. The four Recordbench looks can remain as alternates; the proposal makes one of them the family default.
