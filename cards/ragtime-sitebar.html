RAGtime — site bar as shipped

Read from ragtime/app/src/components/SiteBar.tsx. This is one persistent bar. Left: the fluted mark, an EB Garamond wordmark, a tagline and Explorer. Middle: a slot where a route puts its own controls. Right: Docs, Feedback and AI access. On a narrow screen it wraps instead of collapsing. The chrome mixes two systems: the bar paints lawfare-paper, and its buttons are shadcn (neutral grey border, white fill, Lato).

Gaps against the family

  • Two palettes in one bar: shadcn neutral for buttons and borders, Lawfare warm for the bar. --primary becomes teal, or buttons stop reading it.
  • Lato is the UI face. The family proposal is Inter.
  • No phone form, only wrapping. A two-row Ledger-style layout (brand and tools, then route slot) would keep the order stable.
  • Dark mode is defined but never toggled.
