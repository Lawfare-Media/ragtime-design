Space, radius, breakpoints proposal — not ruled

No two products break at the same width. A phone at 390px is "phone" everywhere, but between 544px and 832px each product is in a different mode. The proposal is three named widths shared by all three, aligned with Tailwind's sm and lg so RAGtime changes the least.
