Recordbench — the Press live · fixture snapshot

The front page after the decision #250 cut (Press.svelte + PressPlate.svelte + lib/press.ts), re-implemented on plain canvases so it can be iterated in Claude Design. The Press is now a normal page: the masthead, then one investigation after another in the document's flow, each with a plate drawn at rest. A plate has no camera, no pan, no zoom, no dive and no open state; its box is the field's own aspect ratio, so the page never moves when it paints. On a plate, documents are bars on the left, passages are dots, claims are rings on the right, and curves join a document through its passages to the claims they support. A claim built on another claim loops out to the right. The field layout is the shipped buildField, verbatim. Hover a point to trace its chain. The draw-in plays once a session per plate (here: Replay draw-in). The descent into an investigation is the page change itself: the plate is named for the router's cross-fade and grows into the overview's header. Investigation names come from fixtures.json.

    The snapshot

    Five fixture investigations. Each entry gives the plate's words, the readings listed under it and the record's totals; constellation is either generated from seed and shape (below) or given literally in the API's shape (documents[{name,length,marks_total,marks[{start,claims[]}]}], claims[{name,rests_on[]}], totals). Paste a literal constellation into any entry to see real shape. Edit, then Apply.

    What was cut (decision #250)

    • The single field. One canvas holding every investigation, with a camera, a dive into one field, a Back strip, the focus lens, the light pool and the arrival over the masthead. Each investigation now draws its own plate in the page's flow.
    • Tokens gone from app.css: --press-ink-3, --press-shade, --press-plate-bg, --press-plate-clear, --press-toner-solid, --press-c-lit, --press-pool, --press-pool-a, --press-grain-tint, --press-grain-amp, --press-night, the @property axes --press-k/-p/-arr/-cen, and --motion-live.
    • The other bench designs. Cards, Desk and Cabinet, the dev-only design switch and every --desk-* token. Press is the only front page.
    • On the bench root: the find bar, the per-row hues, the PlateArt cards and the Plates dials in settings.

    What to iterate on

    • Plate type. Didot titles at 28px (21px on a phone) and outlined numerals that fill on hover or focus. The plate's words sit outside the canvas now, so the title no longer carries the misregistered shadow; only the masthead does.
    • Ink balance. Shipped gains are 1.9 (edges) and 1.3 (nodes) in light, 1.15 and 1 in dark. At rest every edge is drawn at 0.2 alpha × gain; a traced chain dims the rest to 0.55. Claims are the one coloured mark.
    • Draw-in. Once a session per plate, over --motion-settle × 2.5 (2.25s), eased by --motion-ease-standard. None at a motion dial of zero.
    • Phone. Same page, narrower: the plate stays, the lede drops to its first sentence, the numeral column shrinks to 40px, and the agent and gear move to the dock. Hover tracing is off for touch.
