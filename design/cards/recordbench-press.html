Recordbench — the Press live · fixture snapshot

The front page as shipped (decision #241, Press.svelte + lib/press.ts at origin/main 977a820), re-implemented here on a plain canvas so it can be iterated in Claude Design. Each investigation is a field: documents are bars on the left, passages are dots, claims are rings on the right, and curves join a document through its passages to the claims they support. A claim built on another claim gets its own curve, which loops out to the right. The field layout is the shipped buildField, verbatim. Hover a point to trace its chain; click a field or its plate to dive in; Esc or Back to come out. The data is a fake snapshot (edit it below) in the API's Constellation shape. Investigation names come from fixtures.json.

The snapshot

Five fixture investigations. Each entry gives the plate's words and the record's totals; constellation is either generated from seed and shape (below) or given literally in the API's shape (documents[{name,length,marks_total,marks[{start,claims[]}]}], claims[{name,rests_on[]}], totals). Paste a literal constellation into any entry to see real shape. Edit, then Apply.

What to iterate on

  • Plate type. Didot titles with a misregistered teal shadow, outlined numerals that fill on hover. The plate is the only Didot in the app besides the masthead.
  • Ink balance. Shipped gains are 1.9 (edges) and 1.3 (nodes) in light, 1.15 and 1 in dark. Claims are the one coloured mark; everything else is press ink.
  • Phone. Below 640px the stage is not mounted at all (owner, 2026-10-15): the plates become a plain numbered list. See the Recordbench shell card, Proposal C.
  • Not ported: paper grain bitmap, depth parallax while panning, the press-down plate inset, drag to pan when dived, the edge bitmap cache. Each is a performance or texture detail, not layout.
