What the ruling changes in code

AppShell draws no separate bar on the Press page. The masthead row hosts the account on every width, plus agent and settings in a cluster on desktop. Below --bp-phone, agent and settings move to a two-slot icon dock above the browser bar, and Back and Find join the dock on inner pages. The agent icon has two states (connected, not connected). Account details move out of ShellTools into the masthead row, and the dock pads by env(safe-area-inset-bottom) when the app runs without browser chrome.
