Phone — one rule: brand and tools on top, places on the bottom

Rules the bar keeps

  1. The chrome never paints a colour the page does not use. It reads --chrome-paper, which defaults to --paper.
  2. The chrome is set in Inter. Only the wordmark uses the product face.
  3. Height is 52px. A phone adds the safe-area inset and at most one more row.
  4. Icon targets are 32px on desktop and 44px on a phone. Icons share one stroke (1.5).
  5. Tools always come in this order: search, help/feedback, scheme, account. A tool a product lacks leaves no gap.
  6. Teal marks the current place and focus only. Wordmarks and icons are ink.
