RAGtime Family

One design system for three products that sit on the same record: RAGtime (search across the federal record), The Ledger (the RAGtime blog reader) and Recordbench (the investigation workbench). They share foundations and chrome. Each keeps its own voice in display and reading type.

What is here (round 1, 2026-10-15)

  • Colour: every role today in all three products, plus one proposed palette for light and dark.
  • Type: shared chrome faces (Inter and the system mono), with display and reading faces kept per product.
  • Space, radius, breakpoints: eleven breakpoints today reduced to two (phone at 40rem, tablet at 64rem).
  • Family bar: the proposed shared nav anatomy, shown with all three products.
  • Recordbench shell: the ratified shell (Proposal C) on phone and desktop.
  • The Ledger bar and RAGtime site bar: each as shipped, with its gaps against the family.

Recordbench screens (round 2, 2026-10-15)

  • Screen inventory: every screen Recordbench serves, its shell today, and one proposed rule for each place the screens diverge.
  • The Press: the front page's node field, live on a canvas, from an editable fixture snapshot, with knobs for ink, motion and sampling.
  • Components: state pills, kind marks, chips, voices, and the parts that drifted into several (filters, boxes, rows, disclosures, labels, page titles).
  • Product pages, unified: the ten pages inside an investigation, redrawn in one shell.
  • Gates, agent and edges, unified: the bench levels, the agent pages, Connect, Allow, Founding, settings, the phone dock, the tutorial and the data model.

Status

Everything labelled as shipped was read from source on 2026-10-15. Recordbench was read at origin/main after #124 and #125, RAGtime at app/src, and the Ledger at lawfare-web/src/looks/ragtime/reader. Everything labelled proposal is unruled, except Recordbench phone Proposal C, the type roles and the family palette, all ratified 2026-10-15.

Illustrative investigation names in mockups come only from design/fixtures.json in the ragtime-design repo. Its check rejects any other name, because the repo is public.

Considered and not taken

Recordbench phone, Proposal A kept a top bar in the page's paper and moved settings to the dock. Proposal B removed the top bar and put the account in the dock. Proposal C was ruled instead.
