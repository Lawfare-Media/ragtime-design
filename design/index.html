RAGtime Family

One design system for four products that sit on the same record: RAGtime (search across the federal record), The Ledger (the RAGtime blog reader), Recordbench (the investigation workbench) and Investigations, which takes the tokens but has no card of its own yet. They share foundations and chrome. Each keeps its own voice in display and reading type.
